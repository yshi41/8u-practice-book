Session 16 · Week 8Sharpen

Finale

Celebration and closure. This session has one measurable outcome and it isn't technical: how many of these girls sign up again. Everything tonight is pointed at that. Invite the parents to stay and watch.

Kit — balls, goals, something small for every girl, a list of twelve specific compliments written in advance

0:008 min

Arrival — Sharks & Minnows

Where you started. Let them notice how much easier it is now.

0:0812 min

Skills Showcase

Every girl demonstrates the one move she owns, one at a time, with the team watching and applauding. Every girl. No exceptions, no skipping the shy one — help her pick something she'll nail.

0:203 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2320 min

Coaches vs. Girls

Two or three coaches on their knees against all twelve. Lose. Lose badly, loudly, and with theatrical complaints about the officiating. This is the memory they take into the off-season.

0:434 min

Water + parents invited in

Wave the sideline over for the last half hour.

0:4716 min

Penalty Shootout — everybody shoots, everybody keeps

Every single girl takes a penalty and every single girl goes in goal for one. No sudden death, no winners — just twelve girls taking a penalty in front of their parents.

1:0312 min

Awards

Name every girl and say one specific thing she got better at this season — specific, not generic, and written down beforehand so you don't fumble it. "Sofia went from not wanting the ball to asking for it every single time." That sentence is worth more than any trophy.

1:15—

The last thing you say

Ask them, out loud, in front of their parents: "Who's coming back?" Then thank the parents by name too — they're the ones who actually decide.
