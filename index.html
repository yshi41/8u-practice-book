The sixteen sessions

Session 1 · Week 1Teach

Everybody's First Touch

Ball comfort and belonging. Today has one job and it is not soccer: every girl leaves having touched a ball hundreds of times, learned four names, and laughed out loud. Get this one right and you have eight weeks. Get it wrong and you're recruiting for the rest of the season.

Kit — a ball each, 20 discs, pinnies in two colours, four cone-goals

0:0010 min

Arrival — Sharks & Minnows (no-out version)

Everyone dribbles in a grid; two coaches are sharks trying to kick balls out. If your ball goes out, three toe-taps and you're straight back in. Nobody ever stands watching. Learn a name out loud every time you take a ball.

Ramp inSharks visibly avoid the newest girls for the first minute. Let them get away with it.Ramp upThese girls protect a partner's ball as well as their own.
0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — Toe taps & sole rolls

Own ball, own space. Toe taps, sole rolls side to side, pull the ball back, stop it dead with the sole. Twenty seconds on, ten off. Count out loud as a team so nobody's counting alone.

Ramp inAny contact with the ball counts. Praise the tenth touch as loudly as the hundredth.Ramp upEyes up the whole time — call out how many fingers you're holding up.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

Names again. Go around: name plus favourite animal. It's three minutes and it's the reason they talk to each other in Week 4.

0:2520 min

Skill Block — The Toy Box

Four small zones marked by cone colour, each with one task: toe taps, sole rolls, inside-outside touches, stop-it-dead. Girls roam freely with their own ball; you call the switch every three minutes. No lines, no waiting, no turns.

Say this — "Ball close enough to touch every step."  ·  "Find me and tell me how many fingers."
Ramp inBigger zone, slower pace, just push-and-run is a win. Stand with her and count her reps out loud.Ramp upWeak foot only, or ten clean reps before she's allowed to switch zones.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — Team Total

Sixty seconds, whole team, count every touch out loud as one number. Write it on your hand. You'll beat it in Week 3 and they will remember.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 3v3 to Four Goals

Four cone-goals around a small grid, no keepers, three games running at once. Score in any goal. Constraint: after you score, your next goal has to be a different one — which forces heads up and finds the empty space.

Say this — "If a goal is busy, go find an empty one."
Ramp inMake it 4v3 for the side with more new players, and widen their goals.Ramp upMust beat one defender before scoring — no tap-ins.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club

6v6 free play, no positions, no instructions. Put a pop-up goal at each end and let it be chaos — today is not the day for shape. Ask who wants two minutes in goal; take every volunteer. Cheer every save absurdly loudly and say nothing at all about goals conceded.

1:255 min

Huddle

Name one thing each girl did well — every girl, by name, today of all days. Question of the week: "Who made you laugh today?" Pick a team cheer together and use the same one for eight weeks.

Session 2 · Week 1Sharpen

First Shot

More dribbling, and the first shot on goal. The goal needs to become the happiest place on the field before it becomes a place with pressure attached. Manufacture goals shamelessly today.

Kit — a ball each, 20 discs, two pop-up goals, pinnies

0:0010 min

Arrival — Traffic Lights

Dribble on green, slow on yellow, stop the ball dead on red. Speed control disguised as a game, and it teaches the stop you'll need all season.

Ramp inBigger space, calls come slower, any stop counts.Ramp upRed means stop it with the sole and pull it back one yard.
0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — Toe taps & sole rolls

Same as Session 1 — repetition is the point. Add "stop it with the bottom of your foot when I shout."

Ramp inCount her reps beside her; name the number she beat.Ramp upWeak foot only for the second half of the block.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — Dribble–Shoot Gates

Two parallel lanes, one per coach. Dribble through two gates, then shoot at an empty pop-up goal. Girls collect their own ball and rejoin — keep it continuous so the queue never exceeds two.

Say this — "Standing foot beside the ball."  ·  "Look down when you hit it, then look up."
Ramp inShoot from six yards, side-foot, from a stationary ball. A goal is a goal.Ramp upTwelve yards, laces, and beat a passive coach-defender on the way in.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — Golden Goal

One shot each from eight yards. Count the team total. Everyone shoots, nobody waits long.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 2v2 Shooting Boxes

Small box, goal at each end, 2v2. Constraint: shots must be taken inside the box — which drags them toward goal and produces far more scoring than shooting from distance ever will.

Ramp inNo keepers, wider goals, and pair her with a girl told to set her up.Ramp upTwo touches maximum before the shot.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club

6v6, still no positions. Take keeper volunteers in two-minute shifts and start your Keeper Club list on the back of this card.

1:255 min

Huddle

Question of the week: "Who scored their first goal today?" — hands up, everyone claps. Preview the next game in one sentence: have fun, try things.

Session 3 · Week 2Teach

Stop & Go

Changing direction — the first real skill of the season. A girl who can turn away from trouble stops losing the ball, and a girl who stops losing the ball starts enjoying herself. Move of the week: the pullback.

Kit — a ball each, 24 discs, pinnies, two pop-up goals

0:0010 min

Arrival — Body Part Stop

Dribble freely; when you call a body part, stop the ball with it. Sole, knee, elbow, forehead-that-isn't-heading. Silly on purpose.

0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — The pullback

Sole on top of the ball, drag it back, turn the body, go the other way. Demonstrate once, badly and slowly, then let them try fifty times. Do not correct technique for the first two minutes.

Say this — "Foot on top, drag it back, turn and go."
Ramp inWalking pace, ball already stopped. The drag alone is the win.Ramp upPullback and accelerate five yards. Both feet.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — Red Light Corner → Gate Duel

First ten: grid with four cone corners; on the whistle everyone pullbacks and heads the other way. Second ten: pairs at a single gate, both trying to dribble through, using the pullback to escape.

Say this — "The move is for when someone's in front of you — not for showing off in space." (Though showing off in space is also fine.)
Ramp inShe duels a coach who defends at half speed and lets her win the first three.Ramp upLive defender, and she must complete the pullback before she's allowed through the gate.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — Beat the Coach

Coach dribbles, whole team tries to win it. Coach should lose eventually, dramatically.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 3v3 Endzone Dribble

No goals. You score by dribbling under control across the opponent's end line. Why: this rewards exactly the skill you just taught, with none of the noise a goal introduces.

Ramp inHer end zone is the entire line, and she gets a two-yard head start on every restart.Ramp upMust use a change of direction inside the last five yards for it to count.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club

6v6. First mention of positions — just "some of us stay back, some of us go forward." No cones yet.

1:255 min

Huddle

Question of the week: "What can you do today that you couldn't last week?"

Session 4 · Week 2Sharpen

Run With It

The difference between dribbling (ball close, someone near you) and running with the ball (big touches, nobody near you). On a 9v9 field this distinction is worth more than any other technical idea, because there is so much open grass.

Kit — a ball each, 20 discs, pinnies, two pop-up goals

0:0010 min

Arrival — Number Freeze

Dribble; call a number; girls get into groups of that many with their balls, fast. Mixes the group up without you ever choosing partners.

0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — The pullback

Repeat Session 3. Add: pullback, then one big touch and sprint five yards.

Ramp inJust the drag, at walking pace, then a jog.Ramp upWeak foot, then pullback into a change of speed.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — The Highway

Long channels, 25 yards. One big touch, sprint, catch it, again. Two lanes so nobody queues. Race a coach; let them win about half.

Say this — "Nobody near you? Push it and RUN."  ·  "One big touch, then look up."
Ramp inShorter channel, jog pace. She's racing her own last time, not another girl.Ramp upTimed, and she has to beat her own previous run. Add a turn at the far cone.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — Everyone Scores Relay

Team isn't finished until every girl has scored once. Loud, fast, inclusive by construction.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 4v4 Long Field

Deliberately long and narrow, so space keeps appearing behind. Constraint: a goal scored after a run of ten-plus yards with the ball counts double.

Ramp inPlay her side 5v4 and let the extra girl be a free runner nobody tackles.Ramp upTwo-touch limit in her own half; unlimited once she's over halfway.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club

6v6. Keeper volunteers in two-minute shifts — aim to have five names on the list by the end of tonight.

1:255 min

Huddle

Question of the week: "Who ran the furthest with the ball today?"

Session 5 · Week 3Teach

Take Her On

1v1 attacking — the most important thing an eight-year-old can learn, and the skill that transfers most directly to game day. The message all session is bravery over outcome: we do not care if you lose it, we care that you tried to go past her. Move of the week: the inside cut.

Kit — a ball each, 24 discs, pinnies, four cone-goals

0:0010 min

Arrival — Rescue Tag

Two taggers, everyone dribbles. Tagged girls freeze with the ball on their foot; a teammate frees them by passing through their legs. Nobody sits out.

0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — The inside cut

Push across the body with the inside of the foot, change direction sharply, accelerate away. Pair it with last week's pullback so they have two answers.

Ramp inWalking, no defender, exaggerated and slow.Ramp upCut, then explode three steps. Both feet, alternating.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — The 1v1 Ladder

Four small grids side by side, continuous twenty-second duels, new opponent every duel. Winner moves up a grid, loser moves down — but nobody is ever out, so the girl who loses six in a row is still playing and has found the grid where she can compete.

Say this — "Attack her front foot."  ·  "Slow… then GO."
Ramp inThree-yard head start, defender starts passive. Praise every attempt, loudly, regardless of what happens.Ramp upDefender starts live and goal-side; ten seconds to get past.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — Coach's Gauntlet

Every girl gets one attempt to dribble past a coach. Cheer for all twelve.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 3v3, Every Restart Is a 1v1

Normal 3v3 to two goals, but every restart begins as a 1v1 from halfway, everyone else waiting behind their line until the first touch.

Ramp inHer 1v1 starts two yards ahead of the defender.Ramp upNo advantage, and she has to beat her opponent before she's allowed to shoot.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club

6v6. One instruction only: "If you're one-on-one, take her on." Applaud losses of possession that came from trying.

1:255 min

Huddle

Question of the week: "Who tried something brave today?" Take five answers, not one.

Session 6 · Week 3Sharpen

Slow Her Down

First defending — and a retention risk if you get it wrong. New players are frightened of defending because they think it means colliding with someone. Teach it tonight as a skill of patience, not aggression, and start with no tackling allowed at all.

Kit — a ball each, 24 discs, pinnies, two pop-up goals

0:0010 min

Arrival — Follow the Leader (pairs)

Pairs, both with balls, one leads and one copies for 45 seconds, then swap. Deliberately pair a veteran with a newcomer and give the veteran a job: "make it something she can copy."

0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — The inside cut

Repeat, both feet. Add pullback-then-cut as a two-move combination for anyone ready.

Ramp inOne move only, slowly, with you counting beside her.Ramp upTwo-move combination at speed.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — The Shadow

First ten — no tackling permitted. The defender's entire job is to stay in front of the attacker for fifteen seconds. That's the whole exercise, and it removes the fear completely. Second ten: one tackle attempt allowed per rep.

Say this — "Get there fast, then slow down and get small."  ·  "Stay on your feet — don't dive in."
Ramp inShadow only, for the whole block. Staying in front is the success — tell her so by name.Ramp upWin it and dribble out through a gate to finish the rep.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — Bullseye

Pass at a cone from eight yards. Team counts hits.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 4v4 Win-It-Back

Normal game, but a second scoreboard: win the ball back within five seconds of losing it and your team gets a point. Count it down out loud. Goals still count too — run both totals.

Ramp inSix seconds, counted generously, and any touch counts as a win-back.Ramp upIt only counts if won inside the opponent's half.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club

6v6. Praise a good defensive stand exactly as loudly as a goal — this is the session where defending gets its status.

1:255 min

Huddle

Question of the week: "Who won the ball back for someone else today?"

Session 7 · Week 4Teach

Shoot!

Scoring, placed at the mid-season point on purpose — this is the highest-joy session in the book and it lands exactly when novelty is wearing off. Every girl should score repeatedly today. Do not let anyone leave without having scored.

Kit — a ball each, two pop-up goals, 20 discs, pinnies, keeper gloves

0:0010 min

Arrival — Sharks & Minnows (no-out version)

They know it now. Let two of the girls be the sharks.

0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — Foundations & strike shape

Inside-inside touches between the feet, then set the ball and practise the striking position without a full swing: standing foot beside the ball, ankle locked, toe down.

Ramp inJust the standing-foot placement, walked through, ten times.Ramp upFoundations at speed, then strike into a net from a moving ball.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — Two stations, swap at ten

A — Laces Lab: stationary ball, empty goal, ten strikes each, pure technique. B — Rolling Finish: coach rolls a ball in, one touch to set it, then finish.

Say this — "Ankle locked, toe pointed down."  ·  "Watch the ball hit your foot."
Ramp inSide-foot from six yards into an empty goal. It counts, it's a goal, celebrate it.Ramp upWeak-foot round, first-time finishes, or call a corner she has to hit.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — Crossbar (cone) Challenge

Cone on the goal line; knock it over from ten yards. Team total.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 4v4 Shootout

Small pitch, goal at each end. Constraint: you must shoot within eight seconds of entering the attacking half. Removes over-passing, produces a shot every twenty seconds.

Ramp inNo keepers, wider goals, and a teammate assigned to set her up.Ramp upKeeper in, and goals from outside the box count double.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club

6v6. Only instruction: "If you can see the goal, shoot."

1:255 min

Huddle

Question of the week: "Who scored today?" — and if the answer isn't every hand in the circle, fix that before they go home. Two extra minutes, everyone gets one.

Session 8 · Week 4Sharpen

Second Chances

Rebounds and following your shot — the cheapest goals in 8U soccer. Plus the first real keeper teaching: the set position and the bowling roll your no-punting rule makes essential.

Kit — a ball each, two pop-up goals, keeper gloves, a bag of spare balls, pinnies

0:0010 min

Arrival — Traffic Lights

Add a fourth call: "shoot" — everyone finishes into the nearest cone-goal.

0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — Foundations & strike shape

As Session 7, plus one-touch finishes into a net from a rolled ball.

Ramp inBall stationary, side-foot, close range.Ramp upWeak foot, first time, from a moving ball.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — Two-Ball Finishing + Keeper Club proper

Station A: she finishes, and the coach instantly serves a second ball — she has to react and finish again. Station B — Keeper Club: the set position (hands in a W, body behind the ball), the scoop, and the bowling roll distribution. Every girl rotates through B; nobody is assigned, everybody tries.

Say this — "After you shoot, keep running — the rebound is a goal waiting."  ·  Keeper: "Get your body behind it, not just your hands."
Ramp inSecond ball served slowly by hand. In goal, she faces only rolled balls, and every save gets a cheer.Ramp upTwo attackers competing for the same rebound; in goal, live shots from eight yards.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — Keeper Gauntlet

A coach goes in goal. Every girl takes one shot. Coach should concede most of them, theatrically.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 4v4 Rebound Rules

Keepers in. Constraint: no ball is ever dead until it leaves the grid — keep a bag of spares and re-serve instantly so the game never stops and nobody stands around.

Ramp inNo keeper against her team; wider goal.Ramp upOnly rebound goals count.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club

6v6 with keepers. Every keeper must roll the ball out — introduce the no-punting habit now, four weeks before it costs you a goal in a game.

1:255 min

Huddle

Question of the week: "Who liked being keeper?" Write the names down. That's your game-day rotation.

Session 9 · Week 5Teach

Find a Friend

The first "with a teammate" session, deliberately late in the season. At eight, a girl who can keep the ball is more valuable than a girl who passes it away the moment it arrives — so passing arrives only once there's something worth passing. Move of the week: the push pass.

Kit — a ball each, 24 discs (for gates), pinnies, four cone-goals

0:0010 min

Arrival — Number Freeze

Groups of that many, but now each group must complete that many passes before you call the next number.

0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — Push pass & first touch

Inside of the foot, "like a door swinging." Then receiving: one touch that moves the ball out of your feet and into space, not one that stops it dead on your toes.

Ramp inPass against a wall or a coach from five yards. Accuracy over power, always.Ramp upReceive across the body and away from an imagined defender, weak foot.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — Passing Gates

Scatter gates across a grid. Pairs pass through as many as they can in ninety seconds, then rotate partners. Pair a veteran with a newcomer and give the veteran the job: "your passes have to be easy for her to control." That reframes the strong player's task from scoring to serving, which is harder and holds her attention.

Say this — "Look at your friend BEFORE you pass, not after."
Ramp inWider gates, five-yard passes, and the ball may be rolling slowly.Ramp upOne touch through the gate; weak foot only.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — 60-Second Passes

How many completed passes can the whole team make in a minute? Beat it next week.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 4v4 + 2 Neutrals

Two neutral players always play for whichever team has the ball, so the side in possession is permanently 6v4 — passing succeeds constantly, which is the point. Constraint: a goal counts double if three different girls touched the ball first.

Ramp inNeutrals can't be tackled at all, so a new player in that role always succeeds. Start her there.Ramp upNeutrals limited to two touches.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club

6v6. Use the handicap: a goal only counts if a girl who hasn't scored today touched it first. Watch your best players start hunting for your newest ones.

1:255 min

Huddle

Question of the week: "Who gave you a really good pass today?" Let them name each other.

Session 10 · Week 5Sharpen

Pass and Move

The single most-missed idea at this age: girls pass, then stand and admire it. Tonight the whole session says pass, then go somewhere. On a 9v9 field this is what turns eleven passengers into a team.

Kit — a ball each, 24 discs, pinnies, two pop-up goals

0:0010 min

Arrival — Follow the Leader (pairs)

Swap the pairings from Session 6 so different girls work together.

0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — Push pass & first touch

As Session 9, but every pass is followed by a five-yard sprint to a new spot. Build the habit into the muscle.

Ramp inPass, then simply take three steps. Any movement counts.Ramp upPass, sprint, receive the return one-touch.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — Give-and-Go Gates

Pass to a coach, sprint past the cone, receive it back in stride, dribble to a target goal. The pass is the easy half; the run is the lesson.

Say this — "Pass, then MOVE — never stand and watch."  ·  "Run into the space, not at your friend."
Ramp inCoach returns it slowly into her path and calls her name as it leaves his foot.Ramp upReturn pass must be one-touch, and a passive defender is added.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — 60-Second Passes

Beat the last session's number. Announce the old number first.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 3v3 Freeze Frame

Normal 3v3. Twice per half, shout FREEZE and everyone stops dead. Fifteen seconds of questions — who's standing still? who has space? — then play on. Use it to praise at least as often as to correct: find a girl in a great position and name her.

Ramp inOn the freeze, find her doing something right and say so by name in front of everyone.Ramp upThe ball carrier must name her two options out loud before play resumes.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club

6v6. One instruction: "After you pass it, go somewhere."

1:255 min

Huddle

Question of the week: "Where should you run after you pass?" Let them answer it themselves.

Session 11 · Week 6Teach

Make the Field BIG

The most important tactical session of your season. Nine-a-side plus eight-year-olds equals bunch-ball unless you actively fight it — and your build-out line hands you the perfect place to teach the cure. Move of the week: opening up across your body.

Kit — balls, 30 discs, pinnies, pop-up goals, keeper gloves, cones for a build-out line

0:0010 min

Arrival — Number Freeze

New rule: when they form a group, they must be spread out — no two girls closer than three yards. First taste of the theme.

0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — Open up across your body

Receive with the far foot and take the first touch sideways, so you end up facing the field instead of the ball. This one touch is why a girl looks like she has time.

Ramp inCoach rolls it gently; any touch that ends with her facing forward is a success.Ramp upOpen up under light pressure from a defender behind her.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — The Build-Out Drill

Mark a real build-out line. Keeper has the ball; the "opposition" (two coaches plus two girls) must stay behind it. Team's job, every time: two girls sprint to the corners of the box, one goes long. Keeper rolls it — never punts — to whoever has the most grass around her. One touch forward, then look up.

Say this — "When we have it at our goal, we get BIG. Corners and far away."  ·  "Keeper — find the girl with nobody near her."
Ramp inPut a cone exactly where she stands. Walk it through with no opposition at all until the picture is automatic.Ramp upOpposition presses the instant the ball is touched; keeper must choose the correct side under pressure.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — Bullseye

Longer this time — fifteen yards. Rewards the driven pass that width demands.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 5v5 Wide Zones

Mark five-yard channels down both touchlines. Constraint: a goal counts double if the ball went through a wide channel first. Suddenly the edges of the field are the most valuable real estate on it.

Ramp inA girl standing in the wide channel cannot be tackled — a safe, high-value job for a newer player, and her teammates will start looking for her.Ramp upMust switch from one channel to the other before scoring.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club — cones down

6v6 in a 3–3–2 shadow shape with a disc at every girl's home spot. The only instruction, repeated: "Only ONE of us goes to the ball. Everyone else, back to your cone."

1:255 min

Huddle

Question of the week: "How many of us should chase the ball?" They should be able to shout the answer.

Session 12 · Week 6Sharpen

The Other Side

Seeing the far side of the field and switching the ball into it. On a 9v9 pitch, the space is almost always on the opposite side from the crowd — and the girl who learns to look for it is the most valuable player on your team.

Kit — balls, 26 discs, pinnies, four cone-goals, two pop-up goals

0:0010 min

Arrival — Body Part Stop

Add: after every stop, she must point at the furthest teammate from her before dribbling on.

0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — Open up across your body

Repeat, adding a shoulder check before every touch. "Take a picture before the ball arrives."

Ramp inJust the look — turn your head before you touch it. That alone is the skill.Ramp upShoulder check, open up, and play a fifteen-yard pass to the far side.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — Two-Goal Scan

3v1 in a box with a cone-goal on two opposite sides. The three must recognise which goal the defender has abandoned and attack that one. Rotate the defender every ninety seconds.

Say this — "Take a picture before the ball comes."  ·  "If the crowd's on one side, the answer's the other side."
Ramp inPoint at the free goal and call it out for her. Then stop calling it and see if she looks.Ramp upSilent version — no talking allowed, she has to see it herself. Make it 3v2.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — Team Total

Beat the number from Session 1. Tell them what it was. They'll remember.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 5v5 Switch Bonus

Goals are worth two. A pass that crosses from one side of the field to the other is worth one — so a team that never scores can still win by moving the ball well, which is a genuinely useful thing for an eight-year-old to discover.

Ramp inA dribble across counts as a switch.Ramp upThe switch has to beat at least one defender to count.
1:1015 min

Scrimmage & Keeper Club

6v6, home cones still down. Keepers roll out every time, to the wide girl.

1:255 min

Huddle

Question of the week: "Where is the space usually?" Answer: away from everybody else.

Session 13 · Week 7Teach

Win It Back

Defending as a pair, and the five seconds after you lose the ball. This is the session that stops your team conceding on the counter — and it's the one where quieter girls often discover they're good at something. Move of the week: the step-over (for fun and bravery, not for success rate).

Kit — balls, 26 discs, pinnies, two pop-up goals

0:0010 min

Arrival — Rescue Tag

Three taggers now. Faster, louder, and it's already about winning the ball back.

0:1012 min

Ball Mastery — The step-over

Pure fun. Nobody will do it well and that's fine — the point is a group of eight-year-olds trying something flashy and laughing at each other. Bravery is the curriculum.

Ramp inStep over a stationary ball, walking. That's the whole thing.Ramp upStep-over into an inside cut, at pace, against a passive defender.
0:223 min

Water — in shade

 

0:2520 min

Skill Block — Two on the Ball

2v2 in a grid. The rule: one defender presses the ball, the second stands behind and inside her — the safety net. Freeze it every few reps and check: are they side by side? That's the mistake.

Say this — "First girl slows her down, second girl stands behind — never side by side."  ·  "Talk: 'I've got her, you take the other one.'"
Ramp inStart 2v1 so the shape is obvious and the pair always succeeds. She learns the picture before the pressure.Ramp upLive 2v2, and they must win it and dribble out through a gate to finish the rep.
0:455 min

Water + Team Challenge — Beat the Coach

Now in pairs — two girls defend, one coach attacks. Direct rehearsal of the skill block.

0:5020 min

Small-Sided — 5v5 Five-Second Rule
