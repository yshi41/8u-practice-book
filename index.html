Getting started

Session 1Teach

Everybody's First Touch

Ball comfort and belonging. Today has one job and it is not soccer: every girl leaves having touched a ball hundreds of times, learned four names, and laughed out loud. Get this one right and you have eight…

8 blocks · 75 minutes
Session 2Teach

Strike It

Passing and shooting with real pace. They are not too small to hit a ball hard — almost every weak pass at this age comes from the same three faults, and all three are fixable in one session. Plus five minutes to pick a team name.

9 blocks · 75 minutes
